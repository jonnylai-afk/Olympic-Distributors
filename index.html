A legacy of community03

Commerce and community as one continuous act.

For generations, the company's influence has reached beyond the warehouse floor and into the civic life of Vancouver itself. That character — a food business exists to feed people well, and its success obliges it to give back — runs directly into ODI today.

  • 01Leadership recognized with the BC Community Achievement Award for service, charity, and bridge-building.
  • 02Decades of leadership across the community's most significant institutions, including the Chinese Cultural Centre of Greater Vancouver.
  • 03Advocated for the preservation of Vancouver's Chinatown as a national historic site.
  • 04Helped initiate a 72-unit seniors housing project in Strathcona, housing elders in the community the business had served for generations.

A new chapter, the same promise10

Great names don't end.
They evolve.

In 2025, OD International Ltd. was founded to carry the Olympic name forward — not as a traditional distributor, but as a commercial integrator. Six in ten new foreign suppliers fail in Canada within three years — on execution, not product quality.

ODI inverts the model. You keep your goods, your margin and your brand equity; we direct distribution, pricing and channel strategy on your behalf, earning a transparent commission tied directly to your success. Capital-light, scalable, and free of inventory speculation.

You are not betting on a startup. You are partnering with a name that has kept its promises across the Pacific since 1949.
