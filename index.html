Our story

Pioneers arrive before the market does.

We earned our place by honouring commitments on both sides of the Pacific — to the makers who entrusted their brands to a distant market, and to the grocers who staked their shelves on our word. That double trust remains our defining asset.

A legacy of community

Commerce and community as one continuous act.

For generations, the company's influence has reached beyond the warehouse floor and into the civic life of Vancouver itself. That character — a food business exists to feed people well, and its success obliges it to give back — runs directly into ODI today.

  • Leadership recognized with the BC Community Achievement Award for service, charity, and bridge-building.
  • Decades of leadership across the community's most significant institutions, including the Chinese Cultural Centre of Greater Vancouver.
  • Advocated for the preservation of Vancouver's Chinatown as a national historic site.
  • Helped initiate a 72-unit seniors housing project in Strathcona, housing elders in the community the business had served for generations.
